GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

A little about who I am and what I do.

I'm a passionate Full-Stack Web Developer.

I am Juliane Neema, originally from the Democratic Republic of Congo and now living in Uganda as a refugee. Although I did not have the opportunity to attend or complete high school, I developed a strong interest in technology from an early age. My programming journey began when I was introduced to Python, and mentorship through the Code Blossom program helped me continue learning with greater structure and support.

Before moving into technology, I worked in tailoring and other jobs to support myself. Today, I am learning web development and building systems that can help solve practical problems in my community.

I am deeply grateful to my husband for showing me the way and believing in me, to my mentor Alice for her guidance, to Marion for helping me with learning materials, and to everyone in the Code Blossom program for their support.
