About Me

I am Juliane Neema, originally from the Democratic Republic of Congo and currently living in Uganda as a refugee. I did not have the opportunity to attend or complete high school, but I developed a strong interest in technology from an early age. My journey into programming began when someone introduced and taught me Python, which opened my path into tech. I later gained further support and structure through mentorship and the Code Blossom program. I also worked in tailoring and other survival activities to support myself before transitioning into technology. Today, I am focused on learning web development and building systems that can solve practical problems in my community.
